# 🚜 KisanExpress

Know your stock. Compare the prices. Express doorstep delivery.

## 💡 The Problem

Over 70% of rural village farmers struggle to get agricultural inputs (seeds, fertilizers, pesticides) on time — not always because of unavailability, but because:

* **Long Delivery Lag:** Centralized agritech platforms ship from major urban hubs, taking 2 to 4 days to deliver. During urgent pest attacks or sudden monsoon updates, a 24-hour delay can destroy a crop.
* **Lack of Stock Transparency:** Farmers have to travel multiple kilometers to town centers without knowing if the required brand or variant is actually in stock.
* **Unsafe Ledger Systems:** Physical shops depend heavily on traditional manual paperwork, leading to records misplacement, calculation mistakes, and payment disputes.

KisanExpress helps farmers see exactly what stock is available in their nearest mandal shops, compare prices, and get express delivery via local auto/trolley drivers within hours instead of days.

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. **Select Your Location:** Choose your local district and mandal zone from the interface.
2. **Browse & Compare Inputs:** Search for required seeds, fertilizers, or pesticides.
3. **Instantly See:**
    * Live stock status in local shops.
    * Transparent price comparison between multiple regional dealers.
    * Real-time ratings and shop distance metrics.
4. **Place Order & Track:** Confirm delivery location to alert nearby local auto/trolley drivers for express doorstep drop-off within 1-3 hours.

## 🌱 About This Project

This is the foundational step of a larger structural mission: **KisanExpress** — helping rural farmers get quick access to verified inputs while retaining financial capital inside the local district economy. This MVP is a lean, budget-friendly tech layer designed to empower local shops and driver communities before scaling fully.

## 🚀 Planned Improvements

* **Digital Ledger / Smart Khata:** Transparent credit-tracking logs with automatic SMS balance reminders for trusted farmers.
* **Group Delivery Optimization:** Allowing multiple farmers in the same village cluster to bundle orders to save transport costs.
* **Government Subsidy Alerts:** Dynamic real-time integration to highlight subsidized government seed stocks instantly on the dashboard.
